## AI systems

AI tools and agents should not be listed as authors because they cannot take responsibility for scientific claims. Acceptable recognition:

- **Methods section**: model name, version, date of use, prompts, and which sections were assisted.
- **In-text citation**: follow publisher guidance for software/tool citation where available.
- **Supplementary material**: agent configs, prompt logs, and human-edited outputs.
